Choosing a palette for your project

A palette is a small set of colors that work together. Pick one, and your site, app or poster looks consistent without extra effort.

Start with one anchorChoose the color that carries your mood or brand, then let the other three support it.
Follow 60-30-10Use a dominant color for about 60% of the design, a second for 30% and an accent for 10%.
Check contrastText on a colored background needs a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 to be readable.
